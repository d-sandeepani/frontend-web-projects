Good to know

Questions, answered.

Start with the basics, then reach out if you need a plan tailored to your goals.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Programs can start with beginner-friendly workouts and progress gradually as your confidence grows.

How often should I train?

Most programs use 3–5 workouts per week depending on your goals, schedule and experience.

Do I need a gym?

Not necessarily. Some workouts can be adapted for home training with minimal equipment.

How do I get started?

Choose a program and contact the coach to discuss your goals, schedule and starting point.
